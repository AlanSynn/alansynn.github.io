# A Job Title Is a Lagging Indicator

**Status:** English working draft | **Theme:** O. Careers, institutions, and the economics of expertise | **Candidate tags:** career-advice, sociology-of-science

A software engineer's job title can remain unchanged while the work that made the role valuable has been reorganized. The person may once have implemented a large part of a system and now spend much of their time examining generated changes, making deployment decisions, or coordinating work across teams. The title gives us a poor account of that transition.

This matters when we ask which professions AI will change. A useful starting point is to examine the activities that currently give a role its value. Some may become much easier to perform. Others may be combined into a new arrangement. The institution may retain the old title while changing the work that belongs to it.

The opposite can also happen. A new job title can describe a familiar kind of work carried out with different tools. Its appearance may tell us more about how organizations are trying to present their activities than about a fundamentally new form of expertise.

For example, a team could retain the title of programmer while reorganizing its work around interpreting requirements, inspecting generated programs, and responding to deployment failures. We would need evidence about the activities before concluding that the same amount of programming work survives. The profession's institutional identity and the demand for particular skills may move at different rates.

Many important changes may occur before they become visible in occupational names. People may be asked to take on different responsibilities or learn new ways of contributing while continuing to occupy the same positions. The availability of new tools can also change the activities through which beginners enter a profession.

To understand a profession's future, examine the work before examining the name of the occupation. Which responsibilities are being removed, which have become more consequential, and which experiences allow a newcomer to take them on? A profession can undergo a profound change without disappearing from the list of job titles.
