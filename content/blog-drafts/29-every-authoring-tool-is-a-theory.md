# Every Authoring Tool Is a Theory

**Status:** editorial draft / argument development | **Theme:** Graphics, fabrication, and making | **Candidate tags:** computer-science, philosophy-of-science, participatory-design

A drawing program assumes that certain distinctions are worth making. A modeling tool chooses which parts of a shape are separately editable. A motion editor decides how time and movement will be represented. An authoring tool contains a theory of what its user is trying to make.

## Working argument

Consider two animation interfaces. One exposes joint angles at successive moments. Another represents actions, their timing, and transitions between them. The first can give precise control of movement. The second may make it easier to construct a sequence that has a recognizable structure. Neither interface simply reveals an underlying world. Each organizes the world into units that support some operations and make other operations harder.

Generative interfaces introduce another theory through their descriptions, latent representations, and correction mechanisms. A prompt can make high-level intentions easy to express while leaving relationships between specific elements difficult to preserve. The agent might infer many of these relationships correctly. When it does not, the form of correction determines what the user can teach the system about the work.

The essay would argue that authoring research should inspect these theories as part of the contribution. What distinctions are visible? What can be changed independently? What kinds of mistake are easy to notice and repair? When general generators are capable of producing excellent results, a field-specific authoring representation may matter because it gives people a new way to understand and act on their own creations.

## Challenge

A more expressive representation can be harder to learn. Some users benefit from having choices hidden, and highly capable agents may infer an appropriate representation dynamically. The claim is not that more exposed parameters are better. It is that the model of the activity deserves scrutiny.
