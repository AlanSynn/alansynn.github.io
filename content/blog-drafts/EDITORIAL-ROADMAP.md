# Editorial roadmap: 136 individual manuscripts

This is an argument-level editorial plan, not certification that every cited study or every short draft has been verified. The status **Long manuscript** means standalone long-form prose has been written in this revision and source/claim notes are recorded in that file. **Expansion required** means the previous short draft remains. Every row names a distinct claim boundary, a concrete case to examine, and a serious test or caveat. The examples for unfinished drafts are research leads or planned worked examples, not findings. No dates are assigned and no posts have been published.

The initial priority is the relationship between qualitative evidence, graphics authoring, research institutions, embodied state, and education. Read the expanded manuscripts before assigning further work. Do not copy an entire structure or repeat the same future-of-AI argument across neighboring posts.
