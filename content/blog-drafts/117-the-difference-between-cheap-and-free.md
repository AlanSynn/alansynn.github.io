# The Difference Between Cheap and Free

**Status:** English working draft | **Theme:** O. Careers, institutions, and the economics of expertise | **Candidate tags:** sociology-of-science, ethics

A free design program can give two students very different opportunities. One has a computer, time to investigate the designs they create, and access to a workshop. The other has the software but no way to make its results physical. Lowering the price of computation has addressed one of the conditions for participation.

The distinction also matters for capabilities supplied by an AI service. Making a capability inexpensive can open important opportunities. But the capability may be useful only when combined with equipment, knowledge, permission, or access to other people. These complementary conditions can become more consequential as the capability itself becomes abundant.

Imagine a tool that helps anyone design a complicated object. A person with access to a workshop can try several versions and learn from their failures. A person without that access may produce an excellent design and never find out how it behaves when built. The two people have received the same computational assistance. Their opportunities to investigate the resulting possibilities remain different.

There is also a question of continuity. A service that is inexpensive today may become difficult to obtain tomorrow. Someone who has reorganized their work around it may bear a substantial cost in moving elsewhere.

A student may be able to complete an assignment using a service today and be unable to inspect the result when their access expires. Another student may have a way to retain the relevant artifacts and continue working elsewhere. Both initially had access to the same service. The durability of the opportunity is part of the difference between their situations.

A useful account of access would tell us who can begin a project, carry it through the stages that matter, and return to it when a service or piece of equipment changes. The price of an individual capability is one part of that account. It becomes most informative when we can see the activity it allows someone to undertake.
