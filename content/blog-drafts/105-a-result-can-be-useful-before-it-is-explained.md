# A Result Can Be Useful Before It Is Explained

**Status:** English working draft | **Theme:** M. Research as a practice | **Candidate tags:** philosophy-of-science, computer-science

An engineer can discover that changing a component prevents a failure before developing a convincing explanation of why it prevented the failure. The observation can be useful immediately. An explanation may become much more valuable when the engineer needs to decide whether the same intervention will work somewhere else.

The distinction becomes important when the result travels. A practitioner who wants to solve a closely related problem may have a good reason to use the method. A researcher who wants to apply it under substantially different conditions may need to understand what made it effective. The same evidence can be sufficient for one decision and insufficient for another.

Consider an image-processing method that works well under a particular form of lighting. We can benefit from the method without knowing whether it relies on a property of the material or a regularity in the lighting. If we take the method into another environment, the difference between those explanations may become consequential.

If the method is intended for a controlled production environment, demonstrating its effectiveness there may be sufficient for adoption. If the same method will be used under unfamiliar lighting, a better account of the properties it depends on becomes valuable. The cost of misunderstanding the mechanism changes with the decisions someone needs to make.

AI will make it easier to discover procedures whose behavior exceeds our understanding of them. I expect that to increase the amount of useful knowledge we have in one sense, while producing many opportunities to investigate why that knowledge travels or fails to travel.

The value of explanation becomes visible at the point where a result needs to travel. A useful method gives us something worth investigating; a useful explanation helps us recognize the conditions under which we have reason to take it into another setting. Research can make progress in either direction.
